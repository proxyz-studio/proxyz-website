PRIVACY POLICY

Revised 15 November 2026

This page tells you what PROXYZ collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. We aim for a short, honest policy instead of a long one written for lawyers.

Who we are

PROXYZ is a venture studio based in Phuket, Thailand. It is run by PROXYZ STUDIO CO., LTD. You can reach us at hello@proxyz.studio.

What we collect

This site has no forms, sets no cookies and runs no tracking. We do not use analytics that profiles individual visitors. We do not place advertising pixels. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

If you write to us, we get your name, your email address and the contents of your message.

Like any website, the company that hosts ours (Vercel) handles technical data, such as your internet address, when your browser asks for a page. We do not use it to identify you.

Earlier visitors

If you gave us your details through an earlier version of this site, for example to book a call, this policy covers them too. They stay in our work email and our internal records.

Why we collect it

To reply to you. Nothing else.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until there is no reason to keep it. Whichever comes first.

Where it lives

Email and message content stays in our work email (Google Workspace). We do not export your data outside these systems without telling you.

Your rights under Thailand's PDPA

Under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it if it is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw consent to processing at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Committee of Thailand.

To exercise any of these, email hello@proxyz.studio. We respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We can update this page. The date at the top tells you when it was last changed. If a change is material we will mark it on this page for at least 30 days.

Questions

Write to hello@proxyz.studio. See also the Terms of Service.
